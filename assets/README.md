# Star catalog

`stars.tsv` is a filtered subset of the HYG Database v3.8, originally
published by David Nash and contributors at
<https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database>. It includes stars with apparent
magnitude 5.0 or brighter and preserves the catalog's J2000 right ascension,
declination, apparent magnitude, and proper-motion fields.

The HYG v3.x data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
2.5 Generic license (CC BY-SA 2.5). This filtered derivative is distributed
under the same license. The original license notice is included in
`HYG-LICENSE.html`; the license terms are at
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/>.

The TSV columns are: right ascension in hours, declination in degrees,
apparent magnitude, proper motion in right ascension in milliarcseconds per
year, and proper motion in declination in milliarcseconds per year.
